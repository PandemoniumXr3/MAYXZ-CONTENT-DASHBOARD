CONTENT PILLARS

5 core pillars that govern every piece of content. Every post maps to one.

PILLAR 01
THE WORLD
40%
Purpose: Create a visual universe people want to live in. Before they know the brand, they know the feeling. Architecture, coffee, quiet ambition, industrial spaces, golden light.
Audience Psychology: People follow aesthetics first. They want to feel something. This pillar creates desire without selling anything.
Content Examples
  • Reel: 30s ambient morning shoot — coffee, light, silence. No talking. Music only.
  • Carousel: "5 spaces that inspired MAYXZ" — architectural photography breakdown
  • Static: Single frame, perfect composition, Groningen/travel city architecture
  • Story: Poll — "Which city feels MAYXZ?" (Amsterdam vs Tokyo vs Berlin)
  • Reel: City walk B-roll set to one perfect track, branded text overlay
PILLAR 02
THE BUILD
20%
Purpose: Show the brand being made in real-time. Audience becomes invested in the outcome because they watched it happen.
Audience Psychology: People root for what they watched being built. This creates loyalty that money can't buy. BUILDING IN SILENCE series lives here.
Content Examples
  • Reel: Fabric sample arrival — unboxing, feeling the material, no words
  • Carousel: Logo evolution — 10 rejected logos to the final mark
  • Reel: Screen recording + desk — designing the website in real-time
  • Story: "Which colorway?" vote — let audience shape the product
  • Reel: First sample try-on — 15 seconds, face not shown if desired
PILLAR 03
THE PROCESS
15%
Purpose: Show the system and discipline behind the brand. Targets builders, creatives, designers. Builds massive credibility.
Audience Psychology: Creatives follow other creatives who show their work honestly. This attracts the most loyal and engaged followers.
  • Reel: ADHD system breakdown — how you structure your day to build
  • Carousel: "How I write copy for MAYXZ" — actual creative process
  • Reel: Figma session — designing a drop layout, sped up, with audio
  • Story: Q&A — "ask me anything about building MAYXZ"
  • Reel: Morning planning ritual — notebook, coffee, tasks, 30 seconds
PILLAR 04
THE CULTURE
15%
Purpose: Position MAYXZ within a wider cultural conversation. Reference films, music, cities, architecture, concepts. Build intellectual brand identity.
Audience Psychology: People want to be part of something that has taste. This elevates MAYXZ beyond a brand into a cultural reference point.
  • Carousel: "5 films that built MAYXZ's visual language"
  • Reel: City photography walk — reacting to architecture and space
  • Static: Quote from unexpected source paired with MAYXZ visual
  • Reel: "What discipline actually looks like" — short manifesto delivery
  • Carousel: Competitor analysis disguised as taste curation
PILLAR 05
THE FOUNDER STORY
10%
Purpose: The deepest layer. Used strategically, not constantly. CPTSD, ADHD, ARFID, addiction overcome — never spoken directly. Shown through the work, the discipline, the transformation. People who relate will feel seen without you ever having to explain. This creates the most powerful emotional connection possible.
The Rule: Never perform vulnerability. Let the work be the proof. Drop a detail once per week maximum. Let the audience connect the dots. The mystery is the power.
What to show
  • 5:30am desk shot with coffee — no explanation needed
  • "Building when I had nothing" — one line, no elaboration
  • Text overlay: "Year one. Nobody watching." Raw footage.
  • CHAPTERS series — one per week, 30–60 seconds
What NOT to show
  • Never say "I struggled with addiction"
  • Never make a post about mental health directly
  • Never preach recovery or use motivational language
  • Never ask for sympathy — only show results
Story Angles
  • "The person who builds MAYXZ is building himself too."
  • ADHD shown as hyperfocus, not a diagnosis
  • Silence as a former coping mechanism, now a superpower
  • Every product is proof something was overcome

EDITING GUIDE

How to edit every MAYXZ video. Exact settings. Exact process.

The look in 5 settings (Lightroom/CapCut):
Exposure: -0.3 to -0.5 (slightly underexposed, moodier) · Contrast: +20 · Highlights: -40 · Shadows: +15 (lifted, cinematic) · Whites: -20 · Blacks: -10 · Saturation: -15 to -25 (desaturated) · Vibrance: -10 · Temperature: +8 (warmer) · Tint: +3 (slight green push for gritty feel)
Reel Editing Steps — CapCut / Premiere
01
Import + Cull
Import all clips. Watch everything once. Delete anything below 8/10 quality. Be ruthless. Bad clips kill reels.
Target: Keep 30–40% of what you filmed.
02
Choose Your Music First
Music determines the edit. Find the track before you cut anything. The beat tells you where to cut.
Sources: Artlist, Epidemic Sound, CapCut library. Cinematic, minimal, no lyrics for ambient reels.
03
Cut to the Beat
Every clip change happens ON a beat hit or between beats. Never random. The rhythm creates the feeling.
EARLY series: slow cuts (3–5s per clip). PROCESS: fast cuts (0.5–1.5s). PCC: 1 word per cut.
04
Color Grade — Apply Preset
Apply MAYXZ preset to all clips. Adjust exposure clip-by-clip if lighting is inconsistent. Consistency > perfection.
Save preset once. Apply everywhere. Never skip this.
05
Text Overlays
Font: Unbounded or similar geometric sans-serif. Size: large. Color: white or bone. Position: bottom left or centered. Never top. Never right.
Max 5–7 words per overlay. Stay on screen for 1–2 beats. Fade in only — no bounce, no effects.
06
Hook Frame — First 1–3 Seconds
First frame must stop the scroll. Best shot first OR text hook first, then footage. Never start with a boring wide shot.
Rule: If you wouldn't stop scrolling for the first frame, cut it and use a better one.
07
End Card
Last 1–2 seconds: MAYXZ logo or text on dark/black background. Consistent on every reel. Builds brand recognition.
Simple fade to black → logo. No animation needed.
08
Export Settings
1080×1920 (9:16). 30fps for ambient. 60fps for action/walking. Quality: Highest. Always export at full quality, then let platform compress.
CapCut: 1080p export. File under 500MB. Check on phone screen before posting.
Audio Rules
AMBIENT REELS (EARLY, WORLD)
  • Music vol: 70–80%. Subtle, present but not dominant.
  • No voiceover. Let the visuals and music carry it.
  • Natural ambient sound optional: city noise under music
  • Beat drop = reveal moment or best shot
PROCESS + STORY REELS
  • Voiceover or text: always one or the other, not both
  • If voiceover: calm, slow, deliberate pacing. No hype.
  • Music lower: 40–60% when speaking
  • Keyboard/ambient sound is content, don't remove it
