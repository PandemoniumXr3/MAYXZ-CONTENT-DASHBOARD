Section 10

FILMING OPERATING SYSTEM

Exact protocols. Never go to a shoot without a plan. Never leave without enough content.

Before You Leave
  • Phone 100% charged or camera battery full
  • Gimbal charged and calibrated
  • Check shot list — which categories need replenishment?
  • Scout location on Google Maps — find best light angles
  • Earphones in = people leave you alone
  • Wear MAYXZ or minimal clothing — you may be in the shot
During the Shoot
  • Film more than you need. Always. Delete later.
  • Every shot: minimum 3 angles before moving on
  • Check playback after important shots
  • Never color adjust on location — shoot raw, grade later
  • ADHD tip: if focus is lost, do 5 coffee/architecture shots. Routine kills the block.
  • Film transitions — walking, arriving, entering spaces
After the Shoot
  • Same day: transfer all footage to organized folders
  • Folder structure: DATE / CATEGORY / STATUS (raw/selected/edit)
  • Cull immediately while memory is fresh
  • Tag best 10 clips with star marker
  • Back up to cloud before sleeping — losing footage is catastrophic

8-Step Reel Edit Process
01
Import + Cull
Import all clips. Watch every one. Delete anything below 8/10. Be ruthless — one bad clip kills the whole reel.
02
Music First
Choose the track before touching clips. Music determines the edit rhythm. The beat tells you where to cut. Sources: Artlist, Epidemic Sound, CapCut free.
03
Build Sequence
Lay clips to timeline. Gut-feeling order first. Then refine for arc: hook → build → reveal. EARLY: slow (3–5s/clip). PROCESS: medium (1–2s). PCC: fast (0.5s).
04
Cut to the Beat
Every clip change happens ON a beat hit. Never random. Use CapCut beat marker tool. This single step separates good reels from great ones.
05
Color Grade
Apply MAYXZ preset to ALL clips. If one clip can't be graded to match, cut it. Visual consistency > saving a clip. Consistency over perfection.
06
Text Overlays
Font: Unbounded Bold. Uppercase. White or bone. Bottom-left position. Max 7 words per overlay. Fade in only — zero bounce, zero spring, zero effects.
07
Hook Frame (First 1–3s)
If you wouldn't stop scrolling for this first frame, cut it. Best shot first OR text hook first. The first frame is the most important frame in the reel.
08
Export + Phone Check
1080×1920. 30fps ambient. Always preview on phone — the editor view lies. What looks good on laptop often looks off on mobile. Text readable? Colors correct?

Section 22

THE 20 LAWS OF MAYXZ

Not guidelines. Laws. Break one and the brand weakens. Every decision gets measured against these.

I
The documentary is the product.
The content is not marketing for the brand. The content IS the brand. Every reel, story, and post is an episode of the documentary. The clothing is evidence that the transformation happened.
II
Reality is the advantage.
Every large brand has to manufacture authenticity. MAYXZ has it by default. Never fake the apartment, the budget, the struggle. The real is more valuable than any illusion.
III
Ownership over victimhood. Always.
Not: "look what happened to me." Only ever: "look what I'm building now." The past is referenced only as context for the build. Never as an excuse. Never as a sympathy play.
IV
Silence is a brand decision.
MAYXZ does not explain itself. Does not justify decisions. Does not respond to criticism with volume. Silence where other brands would speak. Speak where other brands stay silent.
V
Never announce. Show.
"I'm working on something big" is weak. Show the laptop screen. Show the fabric. Show the notebook. The audience should figure out what's happening before you tell them. Mystery over announcements.
VI
70/20/10. Every week. No exceptions.
70% world and lifestyle. 20% brand building. 10% deep personal story. Shift this ratio and the brand collapses into either a vlog or a product catalogue. The tension between them is what makes it work.
VII
Quality over frequency. Consistency over quality.
Three great posts per week beats seven mediocre ones. But three mediocre posts per week beats complete silence. The hierarchy: consistent mediocre < consistent great. Never: inconsistent great.
VIII
Never sell before trust.
Minimum 15–30 posts of world building before the first product mention. The audience must be invested in the founder and the mission before they are asked to invest in the product. Reverse this and lose both.
IX
Score before you post. 70 minimum.
Run every piece through the scoring system. Below 70 means the content harms more than helps — it trains the algorithm and audience to expect less. Nothing below 70. This rule has no exceptions and no moods.
X
The grid is the first impression.
When someone visits the profile for the first time, they see 9 posts. All 9 must be cohesive, visually consistent, and worth following for. The grid is a portfolio, not a feed.
XI
REBUILD cannot be staged.
A restaged REBUILD episode is the same as lying to the audience. Film the moment when it happens. Keep the phone nearby. The bad days matter as much as the wins. The unscripted voiceover is the whole point.
XII
The preset is law.
Every post gets the MAYXZ color grade. Every single one. If a clip can't be graded to match, cut it. One off-color post in a grid of consistency destroys the visual world being built.
XIII
CHAPTERS is earned, not scheduled.
A CHAPTERS post only lands when the audience has built trust. Drop it too early and it reads as attention-seeking. At 30 posts in, the same words hit like a revelation. Timing is the whole strategy.
XIV
Never copy. Absorb, then create.
Study Represent, Corteiz, Fear of God, Rick Owens. Understand why they work. Then forget the references and create from the understanding. Imitation is obvious. Influence is invisible.
XV
Wednesday is the week.
Film day is sacred. Cancel other things. Protect it. The batch filmed on Wednesday supplies the next two weeks of content. A skipped film day means two weeks of scrambling and lower quality.
XVI
Every comment is a conversation, not a metric.
Reply to every comment like a person, not a brand. Brands leave generic replies. Founders have real conversations. The difference is why early-stage communities become loyal ones.
XVII
Build the library before you need it.
The goal is 200+ clips in the library before the first major push. Consistency requires inventory. Posting from an empty library means forced content. Forced content breaks the world.
XVIII
Sunday is for analysis. Not content.
20 minutes of analytics. One sentence of insight. One adjustment for next week. Then rest. The brand needs you to function long-term more than it needs one more post on Sunday.
XIX
ADHD is an asset. Manage the liability.
Hyperfocus built this OS. Impulsivity will destroy it. Use the hyperfocus states for deep work. Build systems for the low states. The MAYXZ operating system exists specifically because of this — a structure that works whether the brain cooperates or not.
XX
The brand is proof of life.
Every day MAYXZ gets built is a day the founder proved something. Not to the audience. Not to investors. To himself. The content is documentation. The products are artifacts. The brand is proof that rebuilding is possible — and real — and worth watching. That is why this works. That is why it will keep working.
